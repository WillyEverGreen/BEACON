Is This Enough?
Short answer: For most projects, YES. For a next-gen tool better than Lighthouse, you need to go further.

--- TABLE ---
✅  What You Have (Strong) | WCAG A / AA / AAA coverage | HTML semantics & keyboard a11y | Forms, ARIA, color contrast | Media, images, testing strategy | Rule engine references (axe-core) || ❌  Still Missing (For Next-Gen Tool) | Cognitive load & readability scoring | SPA / React dynamic app issues | AI-based semantic alt text quality | Context-aware label evaluation | Low-end device & mobile usability
--- END TABLE ---

💡  Your Differentiator:
Combine WCAG rules + COGA cognitive checks + WebAIM real-user data + AI semantic understanding (your RAG layer). That stack is what separates a checklist tool from a platform.
